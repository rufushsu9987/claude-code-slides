PLATFORM THESIS / 2026

Make the safe path
the fast path.

A shared AI platform lets product teams ship useful experiences without rebuilding governance, data access, and operations every time.

Cloud · Data · Security · AI →
Open with the tension: teams are asked to move fast and remain safe, but today those goals often arrive as separate workflows. The platform thesis is that the safe path should also be the easiest path.

01 / CURRENT FRICTION

REPEATED WORK

Repeated controls become operating risk.

Identity, prompt safety, data access, evaluation, and observability are rebuilt with different quality and cost in every project.

5× common controls
duplication compounds02
The issue is not that product teams are careless. The organization has not yet provided reusable capabilities. Repetition creates slower delivery, uneven risk, fragmented observability, and hidden cost.

Explain the ownership boundary. Product teams still own the user outcome. The platform owns reusable developer capabilities. The cloud foundation owns durable enterprise controls. Governance is encoded into the path rather than added as a separate final gate.

The platform should feel like a product: opinionated defaults, escape hatches, clear ownership, and measurable reliability.

> NEXT 90 DAYS

THE DECISION

Pilot one reusable path,
then expand from evidence.

Choose one high-value workflow and measure delivery speed, quality, risk, adoption, and cost before broad platform investment.

$ start-pilot --owner platform-team --review 90d
Ask for a concrete pilot, owner, success measures, and a 90-day review. Avoid asking the room to approve an entire platform roadmap before the first reusable path has produced evidence.
